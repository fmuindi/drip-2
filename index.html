The 8 Wastes: Which Ones Are Hiding in Your Restaurant?

A practical TIMWOODY guide from Evans. At ENGAGE we played with it. Here’s the version you can use on your very next shift.

Download the Free TIMWOODY Guide

Spot them. Name them. Fix them.

Waste hides in plain sight. Here’s where to look:

T

Transportation

Moving food, supplies or packaging farther than it needs to go.

I

Inventory

More product on hand than you’ll use before it’s due to go.

M

Motion

Extra steps, reaching and searching during a rush.

W

Waiting

Team Members waiting on product, a ticket or a decision.

O

Overproduction

Making more than guests are actually ordering.

O

Over-processing

Doing more than the guest will ever notice or value.

D

Defects

Remakes, mistakes and orders that have to be fixed.

S

Skills

Great ideas and talent on your team that go unused.

Your 1-shift challenge

Find three wastes before the lunch rush ends. Write them down. You’ll be surprised how fast they add up.
